APPENDIX · LEARNING PREFERENCESVARK, in a nutshell+

VARK is a reflection tool for preferences—not a test that tells a child how they must learn. Many people use more than one approach.

LetterPreferenceIn kid-friendly words
VVisual“Show me a picture, chart, or map.”
AAural“Let's talk it through or listen together.”
RRead / Write“Let me read it, write it, or make a list.”
KKinesthetic“Let me try it, build it, or see a real example.”

Offer all four routes; never lock a child into one. Evidence for matching lessons to fixed styles is mixed. VARK definitions ↗ · Evidence review ↗
